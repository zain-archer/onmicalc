OmniCalc privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-25 · applies to OmniCalc 1.3.0 and later

OmniCalc collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing outside your device.

What the app does with your data

  • Calculations, text and files you enter are processed on your device only: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, no third-party services.
  • Files you attach (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, CSV, JSON, text, images) are read in memory to find the questions inside them. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is kept after you leave the tool.
  • History, memory slots, settings and themes are stored in local storage on your device. You can export them to a file and delete them at any time.
  • No permissions are requested: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or filesystem access beyond the files you explicitly pick.

Internet access

The web version loads its own files once and then works offline through its service worker; the desktop and mobile packages make no network requests at all. There is no account and no server that receives anything from you.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

There is nothing to share: OmniCalc has no data about you. Content you copy or export goes only where you send it.

Changes and contact

Changes appear in this page and in PRIVACY.md in the repository. Support is through the project repository's issue tracker, which needs no personal information from you.
