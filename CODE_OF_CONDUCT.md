# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want OmniCalc to be a project anyone can contribute to, regardless of experience level,
background, identity, disability, or how much free time they have. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and specific. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming someone was careless.
- Respect that maintainers are volunteers and that some features are deliberately out of scope
  (anything paid, anything that phones home, anything unsafe).
- Accept that mathematics discussions get rigorous: if a result is disputed, a failing test case is
  the way to settle it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and when
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Reports are reviewed promptly,
confidentially, and fairly. Consequences range from a private warning, through a temporary ban, to a
permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may face
temporary or permanent removal from the project's governance.
