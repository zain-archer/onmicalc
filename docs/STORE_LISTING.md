## Full description [4000]

```
OmniCalc is a complete scientific, engineering and graphing calculator that is genuinely free: no
subscription, no premium tier, no ads, no account, no sign-in and no paid extras. Every tool is
unlocked from the moment you install it.

It also explains itself. Type a question in your own words — "20 percent of 250", "convert 5 km to
miles", "what is pi", "solve 3x + 5 = 20" — and OmniCalc works out what you meant, says which tool it
is using, and shows the answer with the steps.

CALCULATE
• Basic and scientific keypad with exact fractions, percentages, memory slots and history
• Trigonometry in degrees, radians or gradians with exact values at special angles
• Complex numbers, matrices (determinant, inverse, rank, RREF, eigenvalues) and vectors
• Equation solver: linear, quadratic, polynomials and simultaneous systems, with steps
• Calculus: derivatives, limits, integrals, Taylor series
• Statistics and probability: mean, spread, quartiles, regression with r², normal, binomial,
  Poisson, t distributions, combinations, permutations
• Graphing: plot, zoom, pan, trace, roots, intersections, extrema, tangents, area under a curve

EVERYDAY AND PROFESSIONAL
• Unit converter across length, mass, area, volume, temperature, speed, pressure, energy, power, data,
  time, angle and force
• Engineering: Ohm's law, resistor networks, capacitors, RC time constant, force, work, energy,
  density, pressure, kinematics and geometry
• Money: interest (including continuous compounding), loan payments with a full amortisation
  schedule, ROI, discounts, tips, bill splitting, dates and business days
• Programmer mode: 8/16/32/64-bit arithmetic that really wraps, bases 2/8/10/16/36, bitwise ops

ASK IN PLAIN WORDS
• "how many miles is 42 km", "integrate x^2 from 0 to 3", "probability z < 1.96"
• Definitions on demand: "what is pi", "define acceleration", "what unit is N"
• Typing mistakes are understood and every assumption is shown

READ YOUR OWN FILES
• Open a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, CSV, JSON or text file and OmniCalc lists the
  questions it finds inside and solves them. Files are read on your device and never uploaded.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN, OFFLINE BY DEFAULT
• Works with no internet connection at all
• No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, nothing is ever sent anywhere
• Your history and settings stay on your device and can be exported as a file whenever you like

ACCESSIBLE AND ADAPTABLE
• Ten colour palettes in light and dark, including a high-contrast palette
• Full keyboard support, screen-reader labels, large touch targets, reduced-motion and
  high-contrast system settings respected, layout that adapts to phones, tablets, desktops and split
  screens
• Open source under the MIT licence

No sign-up. No subscription. No surprises.
```
