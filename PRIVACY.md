# Privacy policy — OmniCalc

_Last updated: 2026-09-25 · Applies to OmniCalc 1.3.0 and later_

**OmniCalc collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing outside your device.**

## What the app does with your data

- **Calculations, files and text you type** are processed entirely on your device. They are never
  transmitted anywhere. The app contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no
  advertising SDK and no third-party services.
- **Files you attach** (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, CSV, JSON, text, images) are read
  in memory on your device to find the questions inside them. They are not uploaded and are not kept
  after you leave the tool.
- **Your history, memory slots, settings and themes** are saved in your browser's or app's local
  storage on your device only. You can export them to a file, and delete them at any time from
  Settings ("Clear all data") or from History.
- **No permissions** are requested on any platform: no camera, no microphone, no location, no
  contacts, no filesystem access beyond the files you explicitly pick.

## Internet access

The web version loads its own files once (and then works offline through its service worker); the
desktop and mobile packages are fully offline and make no network requests at all. There is no
account to create and no server that receives anything from you.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Sharing

OmniCalc does not share data with third parties, because it does not have any data to share. If you
choose to copy a result or export a backup, that content goes only where you send it.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will appear in this file in the repository, with the version and date
updated.

## Contact

Open an issue in the project repository (see `README.md`) — that is the only support channel, and it
requires no personal information from you.
